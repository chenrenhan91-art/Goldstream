Make Your Home Your Own

Your home is an expression of who you are. Our whole reason for existing at HK HUNG YAN TRADING LIMITED is to help you make it your own. We love helping homeowners, interior designers, and design enthusiasts discover distinctive pieces for the home that complement each other and transform every room into a warm, personal sanctuary.

Located at 九龍灣宏開道８號其士商業中心地下１０３室, HK HUNG YAN TRADING LIMITED partners directly with specialized artisan workshops, textile looms, and contemporary lighting manufacturers. Working directly with the source, we cut out unnecessary middlemen and traditional showroom markups to bring you curated interior collections at accessible, factory-direct pricing.

All products in our catalogue are hand-selected and inspected by our merchandising and quality assurance team. We continuously introduce unique pieces inspired by the latest in Japandi, Scandinavian, Mid-Century Modern, Coastal, and Traditional interior design trends—alongside timeless classics crafted from solid timber, natural wool, brushed brass, and high-fired stoneware.
