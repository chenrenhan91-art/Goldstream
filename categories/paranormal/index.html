Trapped in the Haunted HotelParanormal

After an accident, Dylan wakes in a hotel in another world—with no way out. To check out alive, he must survive seven nights, complete daily tasks, and obey the rules: never answer a knock after midnight, and never look at the extra person in the mirror. He watches one guest break a rule—and get dragged through the glass. A near-fatal encounter unlocks Dylan’s ability to glimpse the next ten minutes inside one randomly selected room each day. His first vision reveals the hotel’s beautiful, commanding owner, Margot, about to die in her bathroom. He saves her. She confiscates his key card—and insists he spend every night in her suite. By day, he investigates rooms hiding creatures that pass for human. By night, they keep watch together as every knock threatens their lives. What begins as mutual self-interest becomes an attachment neither can afford. But Margot is as trapped as her guests. And as the seventh night approaches, an offer from her missing fiancé promises her freedom—at the price of Dylan never checking out…

The Vampire Lord’s Substitute BrideParanormal

Born without fangs or the ability to heal quickly, Clara has always been treated as a disgrace to her vampire bloodline. Now her sister has stolen her fiancé, her parents have seized her grandmother’s inheritance, and they’re forcing her to take her sister’s place as the bride of Adrian—a vampire lord losing control of his bloodlust. Everyone expects him to drain her dry. Instead, Clara offers her dangerous new husband a deal: she’ll treat his illness if he helps her reclaim what’s hers. What she doesn’t know is that he’s spent years searching for the mysterious woman who once saved his life—her. And the blood that can keep him alive could cost Clara her own life. As treatment draws them closer, Adrian begins to want more than her medicine. Meanwhile, Clara secretly treats patients as “Nightingale,” a mysterious healer sought after by the vampire elite. The parents who rejected her are desperate for Nightingale’s help, while her sister plots to claim the healer’s identity—and steal Clara’s reputation and Adrian’s love. This time, can Clara protect everything that’s hers?

Dragonblood AlphaParanormal

In Dragonblood Alpha movie, abandoned as an infant for lacking a "wolf spirit," Kyle is raised by three Legendary Beasts in the Beast Forest. Eighteen years later, he has not only grown into a fierce fighter but also awakened the hidden power of a dragon within him. Seeking his parents, Kyle rescues Freya, the Princess of the Silvermoon Pack, and follows her to the Crimson Flame City. There, he discovers that Elina, a disgraced slave tormented by the pack's hypocritical Alpha, Alaric, is actually his birth mother. Just as they reunite, the Thunderclaw Pack launches a devastating invasion. While Alaric and the cowardly heir Kent surrender and betray Freya to save themselves, Kyle steps forward, unleashing his dragon-wolf bloodline to crush the invaders. However, a darker conspiracy emerges: the Thunderclaw Pack has allied with the Liches, an evil undead race bent on destroying the world. Facing the terrifying Lich King and an endless army of ghouls, Kyle is empowered by Freya's holy light and the united strength of every werewolf worldwide. Transforming into a colossal silver wolf, Kyle defeats the Lich King and saves the realm. In the end, his father, the ancient dragon Vodar, awakens from his slumber, reuniting their family. Kyle claims his rightful place as the new Alpha of the Crimson Flame Pack and finds true love with Freya.
