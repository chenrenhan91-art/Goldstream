Trapped in the Haunted Hotel

After an accident, Dylan wakes in a hotel in another world—with no way out. To check out alive, he must survive seven nights, complete daily tasks, and obey the rules: never answer a knock after midnight, and never look at the extra person in the mirror. He watches one guest break a rule—and get dragged through the glass. A near-fatal encounter unlocks Dylan’s ability to glimpse the next ten minutes inside one randomly selected room each day. His first vision reveals the hotel’s beautiful, commanding owner, Margot, about to die in her bathroom. He saves her. She confiscates his key card—and insists he spend every night in her suite. By day, he investigates rooms hiding creatures that pass for human. By night, they keep watch together as every knock threatens their lives. What begins as mutual self-interest becomes an attachment neither can afford. But Margot is as trapped as her guests. And as the seventh night approaches, an offer from her missing fiancé promises her freedom—at the price of Dylan never checking out…
