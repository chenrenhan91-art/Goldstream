One Night with My Mafia Brother in Law

Seraphina Vale, the lone heiress of an ancient noble family, marries into the Rourke clan to prop up her crumbling legacy. After a fateful night with a stranger, she only learns at the engagement dinner that he is Dominic Rourke — her fiancé’s older brother, the ruthless mafia overlord who controls the entire underworld. A dangerous forbidden affair begins. Coercion, tests, forbidden longing and pursuit intertwine. Their fates are bound tightly together in this bloody vortex of power.

The Billionaire Fell for His Curvy Chef

Lucy has the talent, but upscale restaurants can’t see past her plus-size figure. With her mother’s medical bills mounting, she applies to become billionaire Oliver’s private chef. Since an accident destroyed his appetite, more than a dozen Michelin-starred chefs have failed to get him eating normally again. At the interview, the butler mocks her—until Oliver tastes her home cooking and asks for seconds. Soon, he insists she move into his estate. He’ll only eat what she makes. Lucy just wants to keep her job. Falling for her cold, unexpectedly vulnerable boss was never part of the plan. As they grow closer, Oliver encourages her to pursue her dream of competing in an international cooking competition. But his fiancée sees Lucy as a threat—and steals her original recipes to set her up for public humiliation. With her career and her heart at risk, can Lucy protect what she’s created and finally get the recognition she deserves?

Trapped in the Haunted Hotel

After an accident, Dylan wakes in a hotel in another world—with no way out. To check out alive, he must survive seven nights, complete daily tasks, and obey the rules: never answer a knock after midnight, and never look at the extra person in the mirror. He watches one guest break a rule—and get dragged through the glass. A near-fatal encounter unlocks Dylan’s ability to glimpse the next ten minutes inside one randomly selected room each day. His first vision reveals the hotel’s beautiful, commanding owner, Margot, about to die in her bathroom. He saves her. She confiscates his key card—and insists he spend every night in her suite. By day, he investigates rooms hiding creatures that pass for human. By night, they keep watch together as every knock threatens their lives. What begins as mutual self-interest becomes an attachment neither can afford. But Margot is as trapped as her guests. And as the seventh night approaches, an offer from her missing fiancé promises her freedom—at the price of Dylan never checking out…

The Alpha's Forbidden Prey

After eighteen years in exile, rabbit-shifter Lorien is forced into an arranged marriage, only to catch his arrogant fiancée cheating on their wedding eve. Framed and poisoned with a lethal aphrodisiac, Lorien flees into a stormy night and throws himself into the arms of a dangerous stranger—the apex Lycan King, Raymond. Following a wild, fateful night, Lorien rushes to call off the wedding, only to be surrounded by his ex-fiancée demanding to expose his "filthy secret lover" on the altar. But when the church doors are blown open, the entire realm falls to its knees: the merciless beast who claimed the little rabbit is none other than his ex-fiancée's adoptive father!

Apocalypse Affair with My Bestie’s Dad

Abandoned in the wasteland by her cheating fiancé, Joan kisses the stranger who saves her. It’s supposed to shut her ex up—until the stranger kisses her back and she forgets to pull away. Then they reach the refuge city, and her best friend Emma runs into his arms. “Dad!” Felix is the city’s powerful commander—and the one man Joan should never want. Now she has to stay under his roof and serve as his daughter’s bridesmaid. Joan tries to dismiss their kiss as a reckless mistake. Felix refuses to pretend it meant nothing. In public, they keep a careful distance. Behind closed doors, they keep crossing the line. Every time Emma confides in her, Joan feels the weight of what she’s hiding: she’s falling for her best friend’s father. When Felix’s ex-wife discovers their affair and plans to expose footage of their kisses at the wedding, how long can they keep their secret? And will Felix claim the woman he loves in front of his daughter—even when that woman is her best friend?

The Vampire Lord’s Substitute Bride

Born without fangs or the ability to heal quickly, Clara has always been treated as a disgrace to her vampire bloodline. Now her sister has stolen her fiancé, her parents have seized her grandmother’s inheritance, and they’re forcing her to take her sister’s place as the bride of Adrian—a vampire lord losing control of his bloodlust. Everyone expects him to drain her dry. Instead, Clara offers her dangerous new husband a deal: she’ll treat his illness if he helps her reclaim what’s hers. What she doesn’t know is that he’s spent years searching for the mysterious woman who once saved his life—her. And the blood that can keep him alive could cost Clara her own life. As treatment draws them closer, Adrian begins to want more than her medicine. Meanwhile, Clara secretly treats patients as “Nightingale,” a mysterious healer sought after by the vampire elite. The parents who rejected her are desperate for Nightingale’s help, while her sister plots to claim the healer’s identity—and steal Clara’s reputation and Adrian’s love. This time, can Clara protect everything that’s hers?

Doom's Choice Decipher Gods To Ascend

Black fog covers the continent. Any living thing touched by it turns into black ash. Survivors can only fight for supplies from the mysterious god-given choice panels. Every option hides a carefully crafted lie set by different gods. Leo, a former construction engineer, awakens the Parse System. He does not predict monsters — he deciphers the will behind the questions and sees the hidden price of every choice. On the ruined Route 66, he walks among greedy scavengers and powerful extraordinary beings. Every decision is a gamble between life and death. Can he crack the gods’ games and ascend?

Mysterious Revival

"My name is Hades. By the time you read this, I will have already died..." A mysterious scroll, an eye peering into the darkness, this is the story of a person who survived.

Fake Socialite Reborn: No More Rich Husbands

Stella Reed faked old-money nobility to marry a rich heir, only to be betrayed and thrown from a private plane. Reborn back before her tragedy, she holds the memories of the future. No more chasing wealthy men. This time, she will build her own empire and crush everyone who hurt her.

Reborn Bride’s Revenge

Selena dies tragically after losing her son Liam. Reborn on the day she enters a contractual marriage with Kane, she swears to protect her child and avenge their suffering. In her past life, her blind trust led to Liam’s death. This time, Selena negotiates power over the manor staff, a personal assistant and a monthly spending card before signing the contract. Just as she seals the deal, she hears shocking news: the woman who saved Kane four years ago has been found. Anna, the mother of the child who destroyed Liam’s life, is about to appear. No more weakness. She will take revenge for her son.
